## Unreleased

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-19

### Added

- Gain Brain links gain controls across plugin instances. GS Meter measures
  peak, true peak, RMS, and LUFS. Pope Scope displays waveforms from multiple
  tracks in one beat-synced oscilloscope.
- Gated shapes amplitude with an eight-pattern step sequencer. Multosis
  sequences effects across 16 rows. Minitosis runs one effect from the same
  library with four modulation envelopes and per-effect settings recall.
- Miff filters audio with a hand-drawn impulse response. Wavetable Filter uses
  frames from `.wav` or `.wt` files as filter kernels. Both offer Raw and
  Phaseless modes.
- HD26 combines a unison chorus with a stereo widener. Imagine provides four
  bands of stereo width and Stereoize controls.
- Satch preserves quiet spectral detail during saturation. Six Pack saturates
  the EQ boost on six parallel bands. Tinylimit provides lookahead peak
  limiting with optional true-peak protection.
- Nap shapes its reverb tail with separate Decay, Width, and Tone curves.
  Warp Zone shifts, stretches, and freezes spectra with adjustable feedback.
- Multosis and Minitosis include a visual curve browser, reusable curve
  presets, MIDI and automation switching for project waves, and external
  sidechain triggering for modulation.
- CLAP and VST3 downloads are prepared for Linux, Windows, and macOS. Each
  archive includes the full 15-plugin pack in that format, illustrated manuals,
  and the shared envelope editor guide.
